Fundamental Unit of Chemical Elements

The basic building block of all matter and smallest unit of an element retaining chemical properties. Consists of a dense, positively charged nucleus surrounded by electrons revolving in quantized 3D energy orbitals.

NUCLEUS: Protons (p⁺) + Neutrons (n⁰) // Mass ≈ 99.94%
ELECTRON CLOUD: e⁻ Orbitals // Charge = -1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C
ATOMIC RADIUS: r ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m (1 Å = 100 pm)
